About NASA

What is Nasa ?

NASA, which stands for the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, is the United States government agency responsible for space exploration and aerospace research. Founded in 1958, NASA studies Earth and space, sends robotic rovers to explore other planets, and operates the International Space Station. Its most famous historical achievement was landing the first humans on the Moon in 1969, and it is currently working on the Artemis program to send astronauts back to the Moon and eventually to Mars. Iconic Space Photography by NASA Telescopes.

NASA has captured some of the most famous and breathtaking images of our universe using advanced instruments like the Hubble Space Telescope and the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST).

NASA Search About It
Evidence of Water on Mars

Rovers sent to Mars (like Curiosity and Perseverance) found ancient riverbeds and underground ice, showing that liquid water once flowed on Mars and could have supported life.
Earth-like Planets (Exoplanets)

Using space telescopes like Kepler and James Webb, NASA discovered thousands of planets outside our solar system, including many in the "habitable zone" where liquid water can exist.
Age and Size of the Universe

By studying the cosmic background radiation (using the Hubble and Planck space observatories), NASA helped scientists determine that the universe is roughly 13.8 billion years old and is constantly expanding.
The First Image of a Black Hole's Shadow

Working with international observatories, NASA telescopes helped capture direct images and detailed data of black holes, confirming Einstein's general theory of relativity.
Moon Geology

The Apollo missions brought back moon rocks, revealing that the Moon was formed billions of years ago when a giant object collided with early Earth.
Ocean Worlds in Our Solar System

NASA spacecraft (like Cassini and Europa Clipper) discovered that moons like Europa (around Jupiter) and Enceladus (around Saturn) have vast, liquid salt oceans under their icy crusts.
Everyday Technologies for Earth

Many everyday inventions—such as memory foam, improved water purification systems, advanced scratch-resistant lenses, and modern CMOS camera sensors used in smartphones—were originally developed or refined by NASA.
